01Findings

What the market looks like

Five things to take from the benchmark. The rest of the page carries the evidence.

No credible Saudi agency sells brand and marketing to builders and funds as a portfolio.

Every agency we found sells to one company per engagement, or does brand architecture for large corporate groups. The only site that claims the VC niche, Saudi Tech Startups, names no team and no clients. Orbit, priced as a base plus a rate per venture, has no direct competitor today.

A

The real fight is over funded fintech ventures.

Minto, The Digital Bunch and Founders Makers have all published Saudi fintech brand work since 2023: Enjaz, Tweeq, MyFatoorah, Tamam, Moyasar. This is what Ground sells, and they can show proof that DB cannot show yet.

B

The in-house team is the main substitute.

VC platform teams and studio shared services (STV, Sanabil Venture Studio) are what Orbit replaces. They are strong on product, hiring and network, and thin on brand, launch and marketing.

C

Fund PR has a gap in the middle of the market.

The 2026 list of leading PR firms in Saudi names no startup or VC specialist. The financial-communications firms (Kekst CNC, Brunswick, Edelman Smithfield) are set up for large mandates, which leaves round, exit and portfolio announcements for smaller funds without an obvious supplier.

D

The inside-out model already works elsewhere.

SRMG Labs grew from an in-house unit into a Cannes-winning agency. Founders Makers is part-owned by the Founders Forum network and won Moyasar. Being built inside BIM is a credential, so lead with it.

06What this means for DB

Moves, in the order of the plan

Mapped onto the strategy deck: one quarter inside the BIM ecosystem, then outside from month four.

This quarter, inside BIM

  1. Make Wafra a public fintech case study.

    The three rivals closest to Ground win on published Saudi fintech work. Wafra, BIM Capital and Cashin are fintech too. Publish at least one with measured results before the first outside meeting.

  2. Lead with the BIM link.

    SRMG Labs and Founders Makers show that an agency built inside an institution can sell outside it. Present "built inside a venture builder" as the reason to hire DB.

  3. Price Orbit per company from the first proposal.

    One mid-market retainer in the GCC runs SAR 38k to 80k a month for a single brand. Put the in-house comparison (SAR 60k to 90k) on every Orbit proposal.

  4. Claim the search terms now.

    Today the site ranking for "VC marketing agency Saudi" has no clients, and Phable writes the regional ranking lists that list itself first. Publish the ecosystem audit method in Arabic and English and add a page on the new site for venture builders and funds. This is the same GEO work DB sells.

From month four, outside

  1. Open with fund PR.

    A fixed-price Launch package for rounds, exits and portfolio news fills the mid-market PR gap and is an easy first purchase for a fund.

  2. Sell to studios as their brand and marketing arm.

    Sanabil Venture Studio runs product, design and engineering in-house but lists marketing only as "growth support". Position Orbit as the layer above their product team.

  3. Join accelerator perk lists.

    Flat6Labs Riyadh bundles about $64.8k of services and perks into each seed startup's package. A perk partner reaches every cohort through the institution, which is the route the deck asks for.

  4. Enter the awards buyers read.

    Athar has a Best Fintech Solutions & E-commerce Campaign category (Serviceplan won Gold in 2025), and Campaign's 2026 awards added a Saudi independent agency category. Enter the Wafra launch next cycle.
